Lab guide 12

Mock Exam, Remediation, and Exam Day

A full timed mock exam under real conditions, remediation of everything you missed, and exam-day logistics.

10-12 hours Weeks 1-11 complete
Before you start
  • Roll both lab machines back to a clean snapshot
  • A real 2-hour block with no interruptions
  • killer.sh session ready if you have one — killer.sh/lfcs

1. Sit the full mock, for real

Two hours, closed notes, man pages only. Treat it exactly like exam day.

Set the conditions
echo 'Close every browser tab except the man pages and the exam interface.'
echo 'Set a visible 2-hour countdown timer.'
echo 'Write down every task as you attempt it: attempted / verified / skipped.'

2. Score yourself honestly against the five domains

This is the single most useful hour of the whole course.

Tally results by domain
echo 'Essential Commands:      __ / __ tasks'
echo 'Users and Groups:        __ / __ tasks'
echo 'Operations Deployment:   __ / __ tasks'
echo 'Storage:                 __ / __ tasks'
echo 'Networking:              __ / __ tasks'
echo 'Your weakest domain is your Week 12 homework.'

3. Rebuild every missed objective from scratch

Untimed this time. Go back to that week's lab guide only for what you missed.

Targeted remediation
echo 'For every skipped or failed task, redo the exact lab section from that week — cold, then check your work against the guide.'

4. A second mock in the final 48 hours

Confirm the gap actually closed before you spend the exam fee.

Second attempt
echo 'Roll back to a clean snapshot again and repeat step 1. You are looking for a clean pass, not a perfect score.'

5. The proctoring system check

Do this the day before, not the morning of.

What to verify
echo 'Webcam, microphone, and screen-share all work with the proctoring software.'
echo 'ID matches your Linux Foundation account name exactly.'
echo 'Desk is clear: no notes, no books, no second monitor, no phone within reach.'

Verify your work

Run these before you consider the lab finished.

  1. A completed, honestly-scored mock exam with domain-by-domain results
  2. Every missed objective rebuilt and re-verified, untimed
  3. A second mock passed cleanly within the last 48 hours
  4. Proctoring system check completed the day before the real exam
Break it, then fix it

Break:

# Nothing to break this week.

What you'll see: N/A

Fix:

# If you're not passing a clean mock yet, that's the signal to spend one more week on your weakest domain rather than booking the exam.

Why it matters: The single most expensive mistake in this whole course is booking the exam before you can pass a timed mock cleanly. $445 and a month of momentum is a real cost — a clean mock first is what buys you the right to spend it.
